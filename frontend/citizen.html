Himalayan Landslide Survival Guide

Official NDMA safety procedures for hilly and mountainous regions.

⚠️ Early Warning Signs ▼
  • Springs, seeps, or saturated ground appearing in areas not typically wet.
  • New cracks appearing in plaster, tile, brick, or building foundations.
  • Doors or windows sticking or jamming for the first time.
  • Fences, retaining walls, utility poles, or trees tilting uphill or downhill.
  • Faint rumbling sounds that increase in volume as the slide approaches.
🏃 What To Do During a Landslide ▼
  • Stay alert and awake! Many landslide fatalities occur when people are sleeping during heavy rains.
  • If you are in a building: Move to an upper floor if safe, or curl into a tight ball and protect your head.
  • If you are outdoors: Run to the nearest high ground away from the path of debris.
  • If you are driving: Watch for collapsed pavements, mud, and fallen rocks. Never attempt to cross flooded roads.
🎒 Emergency Go-Bag Checklist ▼
  • High-decibel emergency whistle (critical for rescue search teams).
  • LED flashlight with spare batteries.
  • 3-day sealed drinking water & high-calorie energy bars.
  • First-aid kit with antiseptic, bandages, and personal prescriptions.
  • Waterproof pouch with government ID, cash, and emergency contacts.
